How do I track K-beauty bestsellers on Olive Young Global and Amore Mall every day?

Short answer: both stores publish bestseller rankings you can read without an account — the work is checking them every day and keeping the history. OliveYoung Global & Amore Mall Rankings + Reviews reads the rankings by category, returns rank, rank change since the last run, price, rating and review count, and can add the newest reviews with complaint labels. It costs $2 per 1,000 ranking rows, $1 per 1,000 reviews and $0.50 per 1,000 review classifications. No login.

Olive Young Global (global.oliveyoung.com) is the international shop of Olive Young (올리브영) and ranks products across many K-beauty brands. Amore Mall (아모레몰) is Amorepacific's own store, so its ranking covers the group's brands — Sulwhasoo, Laneige, Hera, IOPE, innisfree and others. Watching both shows what sells across brands and what sells inside one brand house.

How to set it up (5 minutes)

  1. Open the tool on Apify: OliveYoung Global & Amore Mall Rankings + Reviews.
  2. Pick the rankings — Olive Young Global categories (Skincare, Makeup, Suncare, Face Masks, …) and/or Amore Mall (most purchased or most clicked; daily, weekly or monthly):
    {
  "sources": ["oliveyoung_global", "amoremall"],
  "oliveyoungCategories": ["skincare", "suncare"],
  "amoremallRankings": ["best_purchased"],
  "maxProductsPerRanking": 30,
  "reviewsPerProduct": 50,
  "classifyReviews": true
}
  3. Set depth — products per ranking (up to the top 100) and reviews per product (0 = rankings only). The form default — top 5 products with their 5 newest reviews, classified — costs about $0.05.
  4. Optional: only your brands. Keep just your brand and competitors; the whole top 100 is still searched, so the ranks stay real.
  5. Schedule it daily and read the results as a table in Apify (Rankings and Reviews tabs), download them, or pull them by API.

For scale: the daily top 50 of 3 categories for 30 days, without reviews, is $9.00; the top 20 with 20 classified reviews each is $0.64 per run. Failed requests are reported with an error row and not charged.

You can also ask Claude, Cursor or Claude Code through the Apify MCP server — "Get today's top 10 skincare bestsellers on Olive Young Global with their newest 5 reviews and tell me which products have skin-trouble complaints." Setup is on the tool page.

How accurate are the labels?

On hand-labelled Olive Young Global reviews (about half with 1–3 stars), complaint type matched 87% on English reviews the tool hadn't seen before and 93% on Japanese reviews. Reviews in other languages are collected but not classified, and not charged for classification. Automated labels can be wrong — check a sample before a big decision.

Other ways, and their limits

  • Check the ranking pages yourself. Easy for a glance, but rankings update during the day and you keep no history of what moved.
  • Read reviews product by product. Fine for one product; slow when you want to compare the complaint mix across the top 20.
  • Olive Young Korea (oliveyoung.co.kr). This tool doesn't cover it — the Korean store blocks automated access. If you already have Korean reviews from another scraper, Korean Review Classifier adds the same complaint, sentiment and motive labels.
