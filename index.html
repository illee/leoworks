About

LeoWorks is a one-person studio in Korea. We build tools that turn reviews, rankings and leads from Korean and global platforms (Google Maps, Naver, AliExpress, Amazon, Olive Young) into labels you can act on: what customers complain about, which reviews need a reply, which leads fit your customer profile.

The tools run on Apify, connect to AI agents through MCP, and are also available as an API. We make Shopify apps and Korean mobile apps too.
